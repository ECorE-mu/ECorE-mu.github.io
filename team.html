Curriculum & Fields of Mastery

Engineering Pillars.

The core domains explored, engineered, and mastered by our teams.

01 // EMBEDDED

Embedded Systems & Real-Time Firmware

Microcontroller architectures (ARM Cortex-M, RISC-V), peripheral drivers (SPI, I2C, UART, CAN), and deterministic RTOS scheduling for mission-critical tasks.

02 // DIGITAL

Digital IC Design & Hardware Description

RTL synthesis with Verilog/VHDL, FPGA prototyping, timing constraint closure, state machines, and hardware acceleration architectures.

03 // COMMS

Wireless Communications & RF Engineering

Signal modulation, SDR experimentation, channel estimation, antenna simulations, and wireless sensor telemetry networks.

04 // SYSTEMS

Systems Architecture & Low-Level Software

Operating systems internals, cross-compilers, bare-metal memory modeling, high-throughput backend services, and automated CI pipelines.
